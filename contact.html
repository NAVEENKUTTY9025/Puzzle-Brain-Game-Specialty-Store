Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our puzzles and services.

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the US. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days.
Do you offer custom puzzles?
Yes! We create custom jigsaw puzzles from your photos and artwork. Minimum order is 1 puzzle, and turnaround is 5–7 business days. Contact us for a quote.
What is your return policy?
Unopened puzzles can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If a puzzle arrives damaged or with missing pieces, contact us and we'll send a replacement right away.
Are your puzzles suitable for children?
Absolutely. We carry puzzles for all ages, from toddler-friendly wooden puzzles to advanced 5000-piece challenges. Each product page lists the recommended age range.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location.
Can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also track your order anytime by logging into your account or using our order tracking page.
