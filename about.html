Our Story

Puzzling Minds Since 2018

MindPiece began with a simple idea: that puzzles are more than just entertainment — they're a workout for the brain, a moment of calm, and a way to connect with others. We curate the world's finest puzzles to bring you joy, focus, and a little healthy frustration.

7+ Years of Passion
50K+ Happy Customers
2K+ Unique Designs

From a Small Table to a Global Puzzle Hub

It started in a tiny apartment with a card table, a stack of jigsaw puzzles, and a belief that the right puzzle can change your day. We sourced our first collection from local artisans and independent puzzle makers, then opened our doors in 2018.

Today, MindPiece is a destination for puzzle lovers around the world. We work directly with designers, woodworkers, and puzzle brands to bring you pieces that are as beautiful as they are challenging.
